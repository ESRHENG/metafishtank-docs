# 9. Growth Engine: Creators, Scouts & Guild Tanks

MetaFishtank's growth model is designed to turn users into distributors without relying on permanent extraction from downstream users.

## 9.1 Talent Scout System

The **Talent Scout** system rewards users who discover and onboard high-quality creators or active communities.

V3.2 explicitly avoids permanent referral taxes.

Instead, Scout programs can be:

- season-limited;
- campaign-capped;
- quality-weighted;
- subject to anti-self-referral rules;
- based on verified contribution;
- bounded by reward ceilings.

Possible signals include:

- referred creator retention;
- Agent quality / uniqueness;
- verified activity;
- community contribution;
- downstream gameplay participation.

## 9.2 Guild Tanks

Guild Tanks allow communities to aggregate identity, contribution and competition.

Possible features include:

- Guild Agent showcase;
- Guild quests;
- seasonal rankings;
- team-based Arena formats;
- creator collaborations;
- ecosystem partner campaigns;
- traffic / discovery slots;
- early access or beta allocations;
- contribution-weighted reward pools.

Guild Tanks create a scalable distribution primitive: communities can bring in users while gaining a persistent identity inside the ecosystem.

## 9.3 Growth Principle

Growth programs are designed around:

**Create → Interact → Return → Evolve → Share → Integrate**

rather than:

**Click → Transact → Claim → Exit.**
