# 11. $MFT Tokenomics

$MFT is designed as a utility and coordination asset for the MetaFishtank ecosystem rather than a guaranteed-yield instrument.

## 11.1 Proposed Supply

**Maximum Supply: 1,000,000,000 $MFT**

## 11.2 Proposed Allocation

| Category | Share | Amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Community & Ecosystem | 60% | 600,000,000 |
| Investors | 15% | 150,000,000 |
| Treasury / Liquidity / Growth | 10% | 100,000,000 |
| Team & Advisors | 10% | 100,000,000 |
| Genesis Community | 5% | 50,000,000 |

## 11.3 Utility

Potential $MFT utility includes:

- protocol and creation fee discounts;
- Agent creation or advanced evolution functions;
- governance participation;
- developer credits;
- Anima compute / API credits;
- ecosystem incentives;
- role or reputation staking where appropriate;
- verified contribution rewards;
- marketplace or premium-tool utility.

$MFT does not represent a promise of fixed yield or guaranteed appreciation.

## 11.4 Vesting Direction

A proposed framework includes:

- **Investors:** 1-year cliff followed by 24-month linear vesting.
- **Team & Advisors:** 24-month cliff, followed by a published vesting schedule.
- **Genesis Community:** final launch rules published before distribution rather than hardcoding an unconditional 100% unlock.

Final schedules remain subject to legal, market and launch-readiness review.

## 11.5 Emission Controller

V3.2 replaces legacy FOMO-trigger mechanics with a configurable **Emission Controller**.

The controller can define:

- maximum budget;
- epoch / season schedule;
- activity-quality weighting;
- anti-Sybil checks;
- per-user and per-campaign caps;
- governance / multisig configuration;
- timelocks;
- transparent dashboards.

The goal is to make incentives measurable and reversible rather than reflexively increasing speculative pressure.

## 11.6 BNB-Denominated Protocol Fees

Where the protocol collects BNB-denominated fees, a proposed allocation is:

- **50% — $MFT Buyback / Burn**
- **40% — Treasury & Operations**
- **10% — Compute & Security Reserve**

This mechanism is a policy proposal and can be modified through transparent governance and operational review.

## 11.7 Economic Principles

1. **Utility before emission.**
2. **Revenue before narrative.**
3. **Transparent unlocks.**
4. **No guaranteed yield.**
5. **Compute solvency matters.**
6. **Developer incentives should follow measurable contribution.**
7. **Beta points are not a promise of token conversion.**

MetaFishtank deliberately avoids reintroducing legacy GameFi mechanics such as permanent referral taxes, fixed APY promises, automatic “double yield” thresholds or whale-optimized extraction loops.
